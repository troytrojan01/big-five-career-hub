## How to prepare for Meta

Meta interview loops often prioritize execution and product judgment. Candidates usually do best when they answer directly, quantify impact, and handle ambiguity with a bias toward action.

## What strong candidates do

- Show how they shipped meaningful work.
- Demonstrate strong communication under time pressure.
- Tie decisions to product impact and scale.

## What to study

Focus on functional depth, the pace of execution your role requires, and examples where you influenced outcomes across teams.
