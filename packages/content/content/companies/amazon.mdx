## How to prepare for Amazon

Amazon interviews are often anchored in the Leadership Principles. Expect technical or functional depth, but also expect every interviewer to test judgment, scope, and ownership through examples from your past work.

## What strong candidates do

- Prepare specific stories with measurable outcomes.
- Tie decisions back to customers, tradeoffs, and speed.
- Practice concise written and verbal communication.

## What to study

Review role-specific fundamentals, then map your experience to principles such as ownership, bias for action, and delivering results.
