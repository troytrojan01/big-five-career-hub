## How to prepare for Microsoft

Microsoft interviews usually blend problem solving, customer orientation, and cross-team collaboration. Strong candidates balance technical depth with thoughtful communication and product context.

## What strong candidates do

- Connect their work to customer value.
- Show learning loops and growth over time.
- Demonstrate partnership across organizations.

## What to study

Practice explaining complex decisions simply, prepare strong stories about collaboration, and study the business context behind the team you target.
