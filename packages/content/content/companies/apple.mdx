## How to prepare for Apple

Apple interviews often feel team-specific. Strong candidates show functional depth, excellent communication, and an ability to work closely with design, engineering, operations, or research partners.

## What strong candidates do

- Show where product taste changed a decision.
- Demonstrate judgment under high expectations.
- Bring examples of collaboration across disciplines.

## What to study

Study the team's product area, refine your portfolio or case examples, and prepare to explain why your work improved quality for users.
